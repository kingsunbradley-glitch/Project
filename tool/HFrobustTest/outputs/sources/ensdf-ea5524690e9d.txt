265SGAc  in the non-recognition of a second state in daughter {+261}Rf which    
265SGAc  would otherwise have provided a vital clue,                            
265SGAc  may have aided earlier interpretations.

265SGAc  with actinide targets which are known to lead to a relatively large SF 
265SGAc  background when compared to Pb-target based cold fusion reactions.
