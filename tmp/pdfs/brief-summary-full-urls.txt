    change, a smaller lmin , and better preservation of the dominant spherical parentage. It
    is therefore difficult, within the present calculation, to explain why the strongly hindered
    direct transition to ν9/2+ [615] should dominate if the ν11/2− [725] state is available.
    The remaining difficulty of the IMP-only picture is the difference between HF ∼ 30 and
    HF ∼ 4 for two transitions having similar gross quantum-number properties. However,
    this is a quantitative microscopic problem rather than an obvious conflict with the se-
    lection rules: similar ∆K, parity, lmin , and spherical parentage do not require identical
    α-formation amplitudes.
    My present qualitative ranking is therefore

                      IMP-only ≳ common parent with different α branches
                            > IMP+JINR as one common α transition

    although the limited statistics do not allow any of these possibilities to be excluded.
    Would this assessment be considered physically reasonable, and is there a microscopic
    mechanism that could instead favor the direct ν13/2− [716] → ν9/2+ [615] transition over
    the nearby ν11/2− [725] channel?
